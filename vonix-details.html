Samuel Kripto is a cryptocurrency trading platform in Indonesia, operated by PT Samuel Kripto Indonesia and part of the Samuel Group. It is officially registered with BAPPEBTI (Indonesia’s commodity futures regulator), Kominfo, and certified with ISO 27001:2013 for information security, making it one of the regulated and compliant local exchanges. The platform offers trading in over 300 cryptocurrencies including Bitcoin, Ethereum, and other major coins, with features such as low and transparent fees, fast deposit/withdrawal processing, and user-friendly security options like PIN and biometric login. The app is designed to be accessible for beginners while still providing useful tools like daily coin recommendations. However, some users note that the sign-up and verification (KYC) process can feel complicated. As with any crypto platform, risks such as volatility and security threats remain, but Samuel Kripto’s local regulation and compliance measures help boost credibility compared to unlicensed exchanges.
